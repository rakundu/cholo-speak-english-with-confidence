Installing Cholo on her phone (Nothing / any Android) — about 5 minutes
=====================================================================
Nothing to download from the Play Store. Cholo installs from Chrome.

1. Open Chrome on her phone and go to the app's address
   (e.g. https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cholo-english/).
2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen") → Install.
   A Cholo icon appears on the home screen. From now on, she just taps the icon.
3. Open Cholo from the icon. Tap ▶ Start once.
   When the phone asks "Allow Cholo to use your microphone?" → tap "Allow"
   (choose "While using the app" if offered).

CHECK THE BRITISH VOICE (important)
4. In Cholo: ⚙️ Settings → "Test voice". It should sound British.
   If it doesn't, or the app says no British voice was found:
   phone Settings → search "Text-to-speech" → Preferred engine: Speech Services
   by Google → tap ⚙️ → Install voice data → English (United Kingdom) → download.
   Then close and reopen Cholo.

CHECK LISTENING
5. On any "Now you say it" card, tap 🎤 and speak. If nothing happens:
   phone Settings → Apps → Chrome (and Cholo) → Permissions → Microphone → Allow.
   Speech recognition uses Google's speech service, which is already on
   Android phones with Google apps (Nothing phones have it).

GOOD TO KNOW
- Internet: needed the first time, for the AI coach, and for the phone to
  understand her speech. Lessons, the British voice (once downloaded) and
  revision also work offline.
- Progress saves by itself on the phone. Don't uninstall the app or "clear
  data" for Chrome, as that would wipe it. (Settings → Moving to a new phone
  has a backup code, for family use only.)
- Text size: Settings → Text size → Extra large, if needed.
- Keep Chrome updated (Play Store → Chrome → Update).
