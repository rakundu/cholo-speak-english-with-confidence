CHOLO – Speak English with confidence
=====================================

This download has two folders:

  cholo-app/        ← upload THIS folder's contents to GitHub (it's the app)
  ai-coach-relay/   ← do NOT upload to GitHub. It's for the optional AI coach
                      (see ai-coach-relay/SETUP-AI-COACH.md)


A. PUT THE APP ONLINE WITH GITHUB PAGES  (about 10 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. On github.com, click "+" → "New repository".
   Name it e.g.  cholo-english  → Public → Create repository.
2. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in EVERYTHING inside the
   cholo-app folder (the files themselves, not the folder) → Commit changes.
3. Go to the repository's Settings → Pages.
   Source: "Deploy from a branch". Branch: main, folder: / (root) → Save.
4. After a minute or two the address appears at the top of that page, e.g.
     https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cholo-english/
   Open it on your own phone first to check.

(Optional) AI coach: before step 2, fill in config.js — see
ai-coach-relay/SETUP-AI-COACH.md. You can also do it later: edit config.js
on GitHub (pencil icon) and commit.


B. FAMILY SETUP (once, before giving her the phone)
---------------------------------------------------
Open the app → ⚙️ Settings (bottom of the home screen) and fill in:
her name, home town, who she's visiting, the UK city, length of stay,
and (optional) the travel date. Test the British voice there too.


C. UPDATING THE APP LATER
-------------------------
If you change any file, also open sw.js and change the first line,
e.g.  const C = 'cholo-v7';  →  const C = 'cholo-v8';
then upload. Her phone picks up the new version the next time she opens
the app with internet on. Her progress is NOT affected by updates.
